Why should you sponsor Berry Fresh?

Our audience is fiercely loyal, hyper-local, and loves supporting Comox Valley businesses that value community, inclusivity, and a damn good night out.

We are currently booking sponsors for our upcoming 2026-2027 shows

and we’d love to put YOU front and centre.

What a sponsorship looks like for you:

  • Logo Placement: Your brand featured on our physical and digital show posters (and our website!).
  • Live Shout-outs: Dedicated, MC shout-outs from me during the show (no boring corporate reads here).
  • Social Reach: Collaborative shout-outs on our Instagram and podcast networks to connect you directly with our followers.
  • Tickets: VIP tickets to the show so you or your staff can come out and enjoy the laughs.

Sponsorship packages start at just $150

Or we are totally open to cross-promotions (like prize giveaways for our audience!).

Are you ready to see how we can get the Comox Valley buzzing about your business at our next show?
