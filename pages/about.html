About Berry Fresh Comedy

Where did we come from?

My name is Mary Wisforden

I'm lucky to have been able to perform comedy for the last four, nearly five years. I'm even luckier to have been born and raised in the Comox Valley

I spent seven years tree planting all across British Columbia, and a number of years traveling around Canada, the USA, and differnt parts of Europe with my Family and Solo!

Bio

Known for diverse storytelling, and engaging energy. ‘Namgis/Ma'amtagila First Nations mixed with British immigrant, bringing new light to being a first-generation Canadian and a First Nations woman.

Delivering bold storytelling rooted in the everyday life of a mother and the resilient Indigenous spirit. Blending “feral housewife” chaos with “deadly auntie” insight.

Tree Planter turned Strawberry Farmer turned Comedian offering a fresh, unfiltered take on race, relationships, and navigating your thirties in this pre-apocalyptic world we call home. Standing out with her raw authenticity and sharp, genre-crossing edge.

Mary was recently selected for The Misko Way: Rising Stars Cohort

Creating space for Indigenous Comedians inside the writers room.
